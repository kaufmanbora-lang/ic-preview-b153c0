Bulk · Grain

Grain Handling

Wheat, corn, barley and oilseeds. We take them off trucks and railcars, keep them in flat storage and load them onto vessels in Chornomorsk and Izmail.

  • Intake from tipper trucks and hopper railcars
  • Storage in warehouses
  • Building up the cargo for the vessel

Laboratory Testing

Sampling of grain at intake and during vessel loading, and of fertiliser during discharge, and laboratory analysis of quality.

  • Sampling at loading and discharge
  • Analysis of grain quality parameters

Survey

Independent Surveyor Services

Hold inspection, draught survey and supervision of loading or discharge when the parties need an independent check of cargo quantity and quality.

  • Draught survey: cargo weight from the vessel’s draught
  • Supervision of cargo work and sampling

Process

How we work

  1. 01

    Request

    Tell us about the cargo: what, how much, which port and when the vessel arrives.

  2. 02

    Planning

    We plan intake, storage and the loading or discharge to the vessel’s laycan.

  3. 03

    Handling

    We receive the cargo from trucks and railcars, store it and load the vessel.

  4. 04

    Paperwork

    We clear the port and customs formalities and send a report on the lot before the vessel sails.
