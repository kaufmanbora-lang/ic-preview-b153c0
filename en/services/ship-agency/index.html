Ship Agency

We look after the vessel in Chornomorsk and Izmail — from arrival to departure.

Get a quote

What we do

The agent represents the vessel in port. We prepare arrival and departure documents, order pilots, tugs and port services, and handle supplies and crew matters — so the vessel never waits.

  • 01Arrival and departure documents
  • 02Pilots, tugs and port services
  • 03Supplies and crew matters
  • 04Reports to owners and charterers

Process

How we work

  1. 01

    Request

    Tell us about the cargo: what, how much, which port and when the vessel arrives.

  2. 02

    Planning

    We agree intake, storage and loading around the vessel’s laycan.

  3. 03

    Handling

    We receive the cargo from trucks and railcars, store it, bag it and load the vessel.

  4. 04

    Paperwork

    Port and customs formalities and a full report on the lot — before the vessel sails.
