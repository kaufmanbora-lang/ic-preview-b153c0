Storage

Cargo Storage

We store grain in port until the vessel arrives, and fertilisers and general cargo once they are off the vessel.

Get a quote+380 67 473 79 79

What we do

A cargo is rarely built up in a single day. As it comes in by rail and truck, it waits for the vessel in storage in the port or outside it.

  • 01Flat storage for grain
  • 02Storage of fertilisers, grain and general cargo
  • 03Intake and dispatch records
  • 04Building up the cargo for the vessel

Process

How we work

  1. 01

    Request

    Tell us about the cargo: what, how much, which port and when the vessel arrives.

  2. 02

    Planning

    We plan intake, storage and the loading or discharge to the vessel’s laycan.

  3. 03

    Handling

    We receive the cargo from trucks and railcars, store it and load the vessel.

  4. 04

    Paperwork

    We clear the port and customs formalities and send a report on the lot before the vessel sails.

Cargo Storage

What we’ll need

The cargo, the quantity, the port and when you expect the vessel. If you don’t know something yet, just say so.
