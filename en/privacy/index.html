Privacy policy

INTERCONTACT

What we collect

Only what you enter in the website forms: name, company, phone, email and cargo information.

Why

To answer your request, quote the cargo handling and contact you about it.

Who we share it with

No one, except where needed to perform the work in port (the port, the terminal, inspection services) and only with your consent, or where required by law.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to process the request and perform the contract, or until you ask us to delete it.

Cookies & analytics

The site uses no advertising cookies or third-party trackers. Your language preference may be stored in your browser.

Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your data by contacting us through the website form.
