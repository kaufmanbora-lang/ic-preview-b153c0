Phytosanitary

Grain Fumigation

Pest control treatment of grain in storage or in the vessel’s hold to the destination country’s phytosanitary requirements.

Get a quote

What we do

Grain has to reach the buyer free of pests and pass phytosanitary control at the port of destination. We arrange fumigation in storage or in the vessel’s hold and prepare the treatment documents.

  • 01Fumigation in the vessel’s holds
  • 02Treatment of grain in storage
  • 03Compliance with phytosanitary requirements
  • 04Treatment documents

Process

How we work

  1. 01

    Request

    Tell us about the cargo: what, how much, which port and when the vessel arrives.

  2. 02

    Planning

    We plan intake, storage and loading to the vessel’s laycan.

  3. 03

    Handling

    We receive the cargo from trucks and railcars, store it, bag fertiliser if needed and load the vessel.

  4. 04

    Paperwork

    We clear the port and customs formalities and send a report on the lot before the vessel sails.
