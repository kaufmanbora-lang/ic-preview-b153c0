Storage

Cargo Storage

We store grain, mineral fertilisers and general cargo in port until the vessel arrives.

Get a quote

What we do

A lot is rarely gathered in one day. While cargo arrives by rail and truck, it waits for the vessel in port storage — accounted for and under control.

  • 01Flat storage for grain
  • 02Storage of fertilisers and general cargo
  • 03Intake and dispatch records
  • 04Building the ship lot

Process

How we work

  1. 01

    Request

    Tell us about the cargo: what, how much, which port and when the vessel arrives.

  2. 02

    Planning

    We agree intake, storage and loading around the vessel’s laycan.

  3. 03

    Handling

    We receive the cargo from trucks and railcars, store it, bag it and load the vessel.

  4. 04

    Paperwork

    Port and customs formalities and a full report on the lot — before the vessel sails.
