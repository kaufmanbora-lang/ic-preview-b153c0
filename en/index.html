Who we are

INTERCONTACT — experience, reliability and efficient logistics solutions.

INTERCONTACT is a port logistics company specialising in the transshipment and storage of grain and bulk cargo, big-bag handling, mineral fertiliser bagging, port forwarding and ship agency. We take in cargo by road and rail, run the warehousing and organise the loading of vessels. We work in Ukraine’s key port areas: Chornomorsk, Izmail, Reni and Pivdennyi. Services

What we do in port

We handle grain, mineral fertilisers and general cargo, bag fertiliser into big bags and store cargo in port. We do port forwarding and ship agency. And if a lot needs laboratory tests, fumigation or an independent surveyor, you can book that with us too.

All services
01Bulk · Grain Grain Handling Wheat, corn, barley and oilseeds. We take them off trucks and railcars, keep them in flat storage and load them onto vessels in Chornomorsk and Izmail. Learn more 02Bulk · Fertilisers Fertiliser Handling Mineral fertilisers in bulk and in big bags. Learn more 03Big bags Fertiliser Bagging into Big Bags We bag bulk fertiliser into big bags right on the quay. Learn more 04Break bulk General Cargo Handling Steel products, pipes, equipment, palletised and big-bag cargo. We discharge it from the vessel onto the quay and load it out to trucks and railcars. Learn more 05In port Port Forwarding We accept the cargo in port, prepare the port and customs papers and liaise with the terminal, customs and inspectors. Learn more 06Agency Ship Agency We act as ship agent in Chornomorsk and Izmail and take care of the papers, pilots, tugs and supplies. Learn more 07Storage Cargo Storage We store grain in port until the vessel arrives, and fertilisers and general cargo once they are off the vessel. Learn more 08Quality Laboratory Testing Sampling of grain at intake and during vessel loading, and of fertiliser during discharge, and laboratory analysis of quality. Learn more 09Phytosanitary Grain Fumigation Pest control treatment of grain in storage or in the vessel’s hold to the destination country’s phytosanitary requirements. Learn more 10Survey Independent Surveyor Services Hold inspection, draught survey and supervision of loading or discharge when the parties need an independent check of cargo quantity and quality. Process

How we work

  1. 01

    Request

    Tell us about the cargo: what, how much, which port and when the vessel arrives.

  2. 02

    Planning

    We plan intake, storage and the loading or discharge to the vessel’s laycan.

  3. 03

    Handling

    We receive the cargo from trucks and railcars, store it and load the vessel.

  4. 04

    Paperwork

    We clear the port and customs formalities and send a report on the lot before the vessel sails.
